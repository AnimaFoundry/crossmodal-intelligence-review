# Review method and evidence limits

Review cutoff: 16 October 2026. Version 1.0.0. This is a selective critical integrative review, not a systematic review, meta-analysis, bibliometric census or prospectively registered protocol.

## Question and selection

The question is when cross-sensory relations and informative variation improve specified machine capabilities under explicit task and resource constraints. Four research passes covered human language/perception, AI multimodal learning, embodied sensing/control, and mutation/adaptation. A synthesis pass checked related reviews, mathematical identification and predator-prey claims. Selection was purposive and iterative, including counterexamples and overlapping prior work.

The bibliography contains 49 cited records. This is a reference count, not a screened-record count, number of independent replications, or claim that every source was read in full. No PRISMA diagram, screening agreement statistic, comprehensive database export, or pooled effect estimate is supplied. Search dates and source availability influence this sample. English queries and accessible records may underrepresent relevant work.

Primary experimental and theoretical papers support empirical and mathematical claims. Related reviews identify definitions, prior coverage, and conceptual overlap; they are not counted as experimental replications. Author-hosted and official proceedings/publisher records were preferred. News and automated third-party summaries were navigation aids at most; they are not the basis of reported empirical conclusions.

## Claim inclusion and reading depth

references.json records authors, title, year, publication, URL, evidence type, actual reading scope and access date. Extra fields retain bounded claims, access alternatives or metadata discrepancies. Abstract-only evidence supports narrow high-level statements, not detailed effect sizes absent from the abstract. Selected full-text passages are not described as a complete proof audit or full experimental reproduction.

Important limitations retained in the manuscript include:

- H02 elicited sensory judgments through language and imagination; it is not a physical multisensory-training experiment.
- H06 is used at the abstract-level claim boundary; no general optimal-integration law is asserted.
- H09's small null result is not equivalence; H10's heterogeneous positive findings are not general intelligence evidence.
- A01's landing-page second-author field conflicts with the original PDF. The PDF names Han Xiao and Thomas Stibor, which is the attribution used here.
- A04 and several CVF records were accessible at abstract/indexed-passage depth; unavailable full experimental details were not invented.
- A11's theoretical information-preservation claims retain assumptions; they do not establish arbitrary sensor-scale learning.
- E02 uses the ICRA paper identity, not a differently authored expanded manuscript.
- E03, E04 and E05 use proceedings years 2023, 2021 and 2025 while noting their conferences occurred in the preceding year.
- E04 is simulation evidence; E05 supplies a relevant tactile-only comparator, not multimodal superiority.
- E08's test-video normalization limits an untouched-test interpretation.
- E09 is restricted to the stated controllable LTI setting.
- R05 and R06 do not establish a universal impossibility or possibility result for all neural world models.
- B01 concerns mutation supply as distinct from per-individual rate; B02-B03 concern particular asexual laboratory systems; B04-B05 concern specified digital worlds.
- B06's historical publication record was inspected, not its full proof. The explicitly stated modern equations and equilibrium calculation are independently derived in the manuscript.
- B07 concerns community biomass scaling, not a fixed individual-count ratio. B08 concerns clonal variation and ecological cycles, not a mutation-rate prescription.

The source selection deliberately includes existing machine-synesthesia and broad multimodal reviews. No claim that the phrase, taxonomy, common representation, sensor fusion, or experimental controls are new is made. No institutional affiliation or scholarly acceptance is fabricated.

## Analytical illustrations and future studies

The six matrix examples are newly constructed using standard linear algebra. The script performs exact rational elimination and computes Gram matrices. Independent checking used two-column minors and the Cauchy-Binet identity. The examples are not energy-matched designs, learned models, simulation benchmarks, or physical measurements. Repeated noisy observations may improve precision in already observed directions even when rank is unchanged. Local neural-parameter identifiability is distinct from predictive usefulness and physical causal identification.

The contact-response study and optional digital-evolution comparison are prospective designs. No hardware capability, minimum useful effect, sample size, model score, or optimal mutation schedule has been validated here. A development-only pilot must establish the physical measurement contract and freeze margins, independent units, budgets, assignments, and stopping rules before confirmatory evaluation. A failed or inconclusive result must remain reportable.

## Reproducibility and rights

Artifact validation checks references, exact matrix calculations, rendered text and publication hashes. Cross-platform reconstruction is judged by readable Markdown/SVG equality and PDF text/page boundaries; binary PDF equality is an additional local check when available, not an assumed cross-platform guarantee. Such checks do not validate the reviewed science. Internal AI-agent checking is not independent human peer review.

Original synthesis and the original schematic are distributed, not source articles, figures, models, data or user attachments. SOURCE_AND_RIGHTS.md specifies provenance and license boundaries. No legal novelty or patent-clearance conclusion is offered. This review remains separate from software-product and invention counts.
